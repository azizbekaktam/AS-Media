## 🎨 **Design System Applied:**

### **Color Palette:**
- **Primary**: `yellow-400` (bright yellow)
- **Background**: `neutral-950`, `neutral-900`, `neutral-800`
- **Text**: `white`, `neutral-400`, `neutral-200`
- **Accent**: `red-400` (for admin/delete actions)
- **Glass**: `bg-white/5 backdrop-blur-xl`

### **Typography:**
- **Headers**: `text-4xl sm:text-5xl font-extrabold`
- **Body**: `text-sm`, `text-lg`
- **Gradients**: `bg-gradient-to-r from-yellow-400 to-yellow-600`

### **Interactive Elements:**
- **Hover**: `hover:bg-yellow-400 hover:text-black`
- **Focus**: `focus:border-yellow-400 focus:ring-yellow-400`
- **Transitions**: `transition-all duration-200/300`
- **Animations**: `hover:scale-105`, `animate-pulse`

### **Glass Morphism:**
- **Cards**: `bg-white/5 backdrop-blur-xl border border-white/10`
- **Navbar**: `bg-white/5 backdrop-blur-xl border-b border-white/10`
- **Comments**: `bg-white/5 backdrop-blur-xl rounded-2xl`
